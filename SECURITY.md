# Security policy

Dhow OS holds company records, documents and payment data, so we take security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hello@dhowpartners.com** with the subject "Security". Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and its impact. Do not open a public issue, and do not access, change or delete data that isn't yours.

We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days, keep you updated, and credit you if you wish once it is fixed.

## Scope

workspace.dhowpartners.com and the code in this repository. Out of scope: denial-of-service, social engineering, and issues in third-party services (Vercel, Supabase, Paystack, Resend) that should go to those providers.
