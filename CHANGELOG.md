# Changelog

All notable changes to Dhow OS. Dates are East Africa Time.

## 2026-11-22
### Added
- Member directory: verified companies can opt in to be found, with optional contact details.
- Opportunities ranked for each company (sector, topics, types, location, deadline) and a Monday "new fits" email.
- Kenya eligibility check on imported opportunities; everything reviewed before publishing; "Not for Kenya" tab.
- Sign in with an emailed link or 6-digit code; show/hide password; one-question company setup; welcome email.
- Settings tabs for company, documents, verification and email choices; delete account.
- Client invoice reminders with a pay button.
- Staff: private deadlines calendar feed, 7am alert email, instant emails for tasks, reviews, uploads and payments.
- Partners: percentage per service, contract status and files; uploads straight to storage up to 25 MB.
- Full terms of use and privacy policy; robots.txt and sitemap.
- New address: workspace.dhowpartners.com.

### Changed
- Verification is one flow: upload, pay, automatic check (free manual review removed).
- Partner documents: only incorporation or CR12, and tax compliance or KRA PIN, are required.

## 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-21
### Added
- Staff workspace: clients, partners, projects and tasks with review, leads, finance and invoices, analytics, alerts, ideas, team roles.
- Member area: opportunities, events, applications, documents, verification.
- Client page, partner upload and review links, Paystack payments, PDF invoices, Excel and PDF exports.
